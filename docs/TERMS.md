1. **Informational & Educational Purpose Only:** GreenClaim Lens is an automated, public-interest software system designed to analyze commercial environmental claims against codified guidelines, including the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) *Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Greenwashing, 2024* and the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) *Guidelines for Environmental/Green Claims*.
2. **No Regulatory or Judicial Authority:** GreenClaim Lens is not a government agency, court of law, regulatory tribunal, or accredited certification body. A verdict of `LIKELY_MISLEADING`, `VAGUE`, or `CREDIBLE` represents an automated algorithmic assessment of published text against codified statutory heuristics. It does **not** constitute a formal legal finding, judicial ruling, or certified endorsement.
3. **Non-Accusatory Copy:** All system outputs, reports, and API responses strictly assert whether a claim *"appears"* or *"does not appear to meet regulatory standards"* based on available evidence. They do not assert intentional fraud, mendacity, or bad faith.
