# GreenClaim Lens — Case Studies in Environmental Advertising Compliance

This document presents in-depth analysis of four representative case studies drawn from commercial consumer packaging in India, illustrating how GreenClaim Lens applies deterministic rules, statutory citations, and non-accusatory remediation recommendations.

---

## Case Study 1: FMCG Personal Care — Absolute & Generic Natural Claims

### Packaging Text
> *"100% natural organic body wash. 100% eco-friendly and green formula safe for the earth."*

### Regulatory Context & Analysis
- **Statutory Framework:** CCPA *Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Greenwashing, 2024* (Clauses on Generic & Absolute Environmental Claims).
- **Compliance Deficiencies:**
  1. **Rule GCL-002 (Absolute Claims):** The assertion *"100% natural organic"* is an absolute superlative. Under regulatory guidelines, absolute claims require empirical proof across the complete product lifecycle or a precise qualifying disclosure (e.g. indicating whether "100%" applies to the entire liquid formulation or solely active botanical ingredients).
  2. **Rule GCL-001 (Generic Environmental Terms):** Broad terms like *"eco-friendly"* and *"green"* without qualifying scope or accessible substantiation fail specificity requirements.

### GreenClaim Lens Evaluation Output
- **Verdict:** `LIKELY_MISLEADING`
- **Risk Score:** `85 / 100` (High Risk)
- **Explanation:** *"This claim does not appear to meet regulatory standards due to high-severity unverified or absolute assertions without qualifying disclosures."*
- **Cited Provisions:** CCPA Guidelines 2024, Rule GCL-002, Rule GCL-001.

### Actionable Remediation Guidance
1. Replace generic assertions with qualified component percentages: *"Formulated with 85% certified organic plant extracts"*.
2. Cite accredited certification schemes: *"Organic ingredients certified under NPOP / GOTS (License: ORG-2024-88)"*.
3. Add on-pack QR code linking to the laboratory Certificate of Analysis (CoA).

### Resulting Compliant Claim
> *"Body wash containing 85% certified organic botanical extracts (certified under NPOP, License #ORG-2024-88). Bottle made from 50% post-consumer recycled PET. Scan QR for full ingredient provenance."*
> **Updated Verdict:** `CREDIBLE` (Risk Score: `15 / 100`)

## Case Study 2: Food & Beverage Packaging — Unqualified Biodegradability Claims

### Packaging Text
> *"100% biodegradable and compostable takeaway meal container."*

### Regulatory Context & Analysis
- **Statutory Framework:** CCPA Guidelines 2024; Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016 (as amended 2022/2024); IS/ISO 17088 standards.
- **Compliance Deficiencies:**
  1. **Rule GCL-007 (Unqualified Biodegradability / Compostability):** Declaring packaging *"biodegradable"* or *"compostable"* without specifying the exact disposal environment (e.g. industrial vs. home composting), expected degradation timeframe, and accredited test standard misleads consumers into disposing of industrial bioplastics in municipal home waste or soil.

### GreenClaim Lens Evaluation Output
- **Verdict:** `VAGUE` / `LIKELY_MISLEADING`
- **Risk Score:** `72 / 100` (Medium-High Risk)
- **Explanation:** *"This claim does not appear to meet guidelines (rules: GCL-007). Biodegradability and compostability claims must specify the required disposal environment, timeframe, and accredited test standard."*
- **Cited Provisions:** CCPA Guidelines 2024; IS 17088; Rule GCL-007.

### Actionable Remediation Guidance
1. Explicitly designate the composting condition: *"Compostable in municipal/industrial facilities within 180 days"*.
2. Cite the Bureau of Indian Standards testing standard: *"Conforms to IS/ISO 17088"*.
3. Include the mandatory Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) manufacturer registration certificate number.

### Resulting Compliant Claim
> *"Container industrially compostable within 180 days in commercial facilities as per IS/ISO 17088 (CPCB Registration #CPCB/WMC/2024/0981). Not suitable for backyard home composting."*
> **Updated Verdict:** `CREDIBLE` (Risk Score: `10 / 100`)

## Case Study 3: Logistics & E-Commerce — Carbon Neutral Delivery Claims

### Packaging & Cart Banner Text
> *"Zero Carbon Delivery: This package was delivered with 100% carbon neutral shipping."*

### Regulatory Context & Analysis
- **Statutory Framework:** CCPA Guidelines 2024 (Clause regarding Carbon Neutrality & Offset Disclosures).
- **Compliance Deficiencies:**
  1. **Rule GCL-005 (Carbon Neutrality via Offsets):** Advertisers cannot conflate direct operational emission reductions (e.g. electric delivery vehicles) with unverified carbon offset purchases. The disclosure must clarify what percentage represents gross operational reductions and provide verifiable registry details for any retired carbon credits.

### GreenClaim Lens Evaluation Output
- **Verdict:** `LIKELY_MISLEADING`
- **Risk Score:** `78 / 100` (High Risk)
- **Explanation:** *"This claim does not appear to meet guidelines (rules: GCL-005). Claims of carbon neutrality must distinguish between direct emissions reductions and carbon credits/offsets, providing accredited registry details."*
- **Cited Provisions:** CCPA Guidelines 2024, Rule GCL-005.

### Actionable Remediation Guidance
1. Disclose direct reductions achieved prior to offsetting: *"Direct route emissions reduced by 40% using EV fleet"*.
2. Cite verified carbon credit registry details: *"Remaining emissions compensated via Gold Standard certified renewable energy credits (Registry ID: GS-2024-IN-492)"*.

### Resulting Compliant Claim
> *"Carbon neutral delivery: 40% direct emission reduction via last-mile EV delivery; remaining operational emissions neutralized via Gold Standard certified solar project credits (Registry ID: GS-IN-2024-884)."*
> **Updated Verdict:** `CREDIBLE` (Risk Score: `12 / 100`)

## Case Study 4: Vernacular Hindi Cleaning Product — Generic Nature Claims

### Packaging Text
> *"हमारा फॉर्मूला 100% प्राकृतिक और पर्यावरण-अनुकूल है जो आपके घर और धरती की रक्षा करता है।"*

### Regulatory Context & Analysis
- **Statutory Framework:** CCPA Guidelines 2024 (Vernacular Advertising Applicability).
- **Compliance Deficiencies:**
  1. **Rule GCL-008 (Hindi Broad Environmental Claims):** Vernacular claims enjoy broad reach across Tier 2 and Tier 3 Indian markets. Terms like *"पर्यावरण-अनुकूल"* and *"100% प्राकृतिक"* trigger bilingual regulatory scrutiny if made without empirical substantiation.

### GreenClaim Lens Evaluation Output
- **Verdict:** `LIKELY_MISLEADING`
- **Risk Score:** `80 / 100` (High Risk)
- **Explanation:** *"This claim does not appear to meet guidelines (rules: GCL-008). Hindi environmental terms such as 'पर्यावरण अनुकूल' require qualifying scope and verifiable substantiation."*
- **Cited Provisions:** CCPA Guidelines 2024, Rule GCL-008.

### Actionable Remediation Guidance
1. Qualify the specific sustainable attribute in Hindi: *"50% पुनर्चक्रित प्लास्टिक से बनी बोतल"*.
2. Cite national standard or third-party laboratory verification: *"परीक्षण मानक: IS-14021"*.

### Resulting Compliant Claim
> *"40% पुनर्चक्रित सामग्री से निर्मित पर्यावरण अनुकूल बोतल (मानक: ISO-14021, प्रमाणपत्र सं: CERT-2024-IND)। सामग्री का पूर्ण विवरण क्यूआर कोड पर उपलब्ध।"*
> **Updated Verdict:** `CREDIBLE` (Risk Score: `15 / 100`)
